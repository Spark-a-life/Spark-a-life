## Decision

Consolidate v1.2 as a bounded Codex execution candidate. It is sufficiently specified to begin target-host discovery and the first fictional workflow. It is not a demonstrated integration, a reliability certification or a proven productivity improvement.

The important change is the decision-to-action connection. SaaP first establishes what is happening, what matters, plausible explanations, gaps and practical options. PaP2 then specifies the selected work, boundaries, checks and handover. The supplied SaaP example already reaches practical action; this revision does not claim that execution is a category above sensemaking.

## 3. Evaluation of the supplied SaaP example

The example is useful because it makes the epistemic sequence accessible: understand the purpose, organise the information, explore mechanisms and perspectives, challenge assumptions, explain meaning, compare options and identify next actions.

Three adaptations were necessary:

1. The instruction to ask a clarifying question applies when a material unknown cannot be inspected and changes the dependent action. It does not stop unrelated discovery or authorised local work.
2. Act identifies the sensible next step. Execution still requires actual scope, permissions and controls; a recommendation is not an authorisation.
3. Flexible presentation is preserved. A connection error needs proportionate diagnosis, not seven ceremonial sections.

The final candidate now tests whether the chosen task is actually important, whose burden changes and which observation would overturn the recommendation. This is a stronger sensemaking contract than a list of technical tasks alone.

## 8. Conditions before relying on the implementation

The following remain open:
- Actual target-machine access and current hardware/runtime inventory.
- One observed local inference path and the selected client's failure semantics.
- Actual source/content fidelity, including contradictions and instruction-like source text.
- Real restart, cancellation and recovery behaviour.
- Will's observed comprehension, correction effort and acceptance.
- Comparable baseline and pilot records.
- Any extension to a new workflow, host, provider or sustained operation.

A small screening pilot can discover failures and indicate feasibility. It cannot establish a rare-failure rate or organisation-wide benefit.

## 9. Strongest counter-case and falsifier

The strongest counter-case is that the existing process already handles this enquiry quickly, and the local model adds preparation, waiting and review. The model may be technically operational while the workflow is economically unattractive.

A direct falsifier of the productivity hypothesis is measured assisted effort, including review and allocated upkeep, consistently exceeding the comparable baseline without a compensating accepted benefit. A material factual omission, false commitment or boundary bypass also defeats the corresponding acceptance claim regardless of speed.

Therefore the next action is not a hardware purchase or broader platform. It is target-host discovery followed by one measured fictional workflow and an honest decision to continue, simplify or stop.
