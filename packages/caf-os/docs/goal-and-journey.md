# /goal and /journey

## /goal

Establish a practitioner-grade conversational production operating system that allows an accountable human director to express creative intent naturally while CAF-OS converts that intent into traceable, testable and reversible production actions.

The goal is not autonomous film generation. The goal is controlled delegation with preserved human authorship, explicit model-routing evidence, persistent identity, audio-first temporal discipline, reproducible assets and governed release.

### Success conditions

A production is successful only when it is:

- creatively coherent with the declared intent
- technically reproducible from registered inputs and configuration
- identity-consistent at the required shot, scene or production scope
- rights-cleared for likeness, voice, source material and distribution
- accessible for its intended audience and channel
- auditable through append-only records
- model-portable, so provider substitution does not destroy the production logic
- approved by an accountable human at defined gates

## /journey

### 1. Frame intent
Capture purpose, audience, emotional effect, constraints, cultural context, rights conditions and publication boundaries.

### 2. Build the production truth
Create story, character, voice, style, shot and rights registries. Resolve contradictions before generation.

### 3. Lock time through audio
Approve dialogue, pacing, pauses, ambience and cue points before expensive visual generation where lip-sync or dialogue timing matters.

### 4. Design identity persistence
Choose the required persistence scope and bind suitable front, three-quarter, profile, body, costume and voice references.

### 5. Route by evidence
Score each shot for character count, pose change, dialogue, camera complexity, motion, occlusion, visual realism, cost, latency and editability. Return a recommended engine, alternative, rationale, risk and confidence.

### 6. Execute asynchronously
Submit jobs without blocking the directing conversation. Register every job and resulting asset under a deterministic project path.

### 7. Review like a veteran
Compare takes against narrative purpose, performance truth, identity stability, visual physics, lip-sync, continuity, sound, accessibility and rights. Do not approve because a render merely looks impressive.

### 8. Correct at the level of cause
Translate feedback into controlled changes. Separate performance, camera, identity, lighting, timing and post-production causes instead of regenerating blindly.

### 9. Pass Captain's Gate
Require explicit human approval for identity, editorial lock, rights, accessibility, export and release.

### 10. Preserve learning
Record accepted and rejected decisions, failure modes, provider behaviour and approved presets so future productions begin with evidence rather than folklore.
