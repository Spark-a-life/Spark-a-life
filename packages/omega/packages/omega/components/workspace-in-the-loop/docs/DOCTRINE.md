# WiseGen Workspace-in-the-Loop Doctrine

## Positioning

WiseGen APE Intelligence is not merely an AI agent framework. It is a governed cognitive operating system for orchestrating, recording, evaluating, and improving organisational reasoning across humans and AI.

## The problem

AI lacks a passport in the institutional sense. It has no sovereign accountability, no legal selfhood, no enduring personal liability, and no stable jurisdictional identity. Therefore the institution cannot safely rely on the model as the accountable unit.

WiseGen's answer is not to pretend the AI has identity. WiseGen gives the **decision** a passport.

## Decision passport

Every decision must have:

1. Initiator
2. Objective
3. Context
4. Evidence
5. Assumptions
6. Workspace state
7. Deliberation lanes
8. Ranking criteria
9. Risk review
10. Decision authority
11. Final recommendation
12. Outcome feedback
13. Witness hash

## Workspace-in-the-loop

Human-in-the-loop is insufficient if the human only sees the final output. Workspace-in-the-loop requires exposing enough structured intermediate reasoning artefacts for review, calibration, and governance.

This repo avoids proprietary or hidden chain-of-thought dependency. It creates explicit, inspectable deliberation artefacts outside the model.

## Non-negotiables

- No final answer without evidence status.
- No ranking without criteria.
- No criteria without weights.
- No high-risk execution without Captain's Gate.
- No decision without witness logging.
- No learning without outcome feedback.

## Practical implications

For LeadGen, this prevents shallow scraping and turns prospecting into strategic qualification.

For Media OS, this prevents attractive but risky content from being published without narrative, factual, brand, and accessibility checks.

For governance, this converts AI usage from performative compliance to inspectable institutional reasoning.
