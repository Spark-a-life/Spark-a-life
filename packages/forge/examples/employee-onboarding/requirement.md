Build an employee onboarding application to replace the spreadsheet the People and
Culture team maintains today.

Today the team tracks new hires in a shared spreadsheet, chases managers by email for
sign-off, and cannot answer questions about who changed what. Personal data including
identification numbers and salary sits in the same file that managers open.

We need role-based access so a manager sees the checklist but not the pay record, human
approval before an onboarding record is closed, complete audit history, PDPA-aligned
handling of personal data, and an export we can take with us. It must be deployable
inside our own VPC, and we must own the code.
