# Onboarding policy (extract)

1. Onboarding for a new employee begins on the date the signed letter of appointment
   is returned, and must be completed within fourteen calendar days of the start date.

2. The reporting manager is accountable for confirming that every checklist item is
   complete. Human approval is required before an onboarding record may be closed.
   No automated process may close a record on the manager's behalf.

3. Personal data collected during onboarding is handled under the Personal Data
   Protection Act. Identification numbers, salary and bank details are visible only
   to People and Culture. Managers see the checklist status, not the pay record.

4. Every change to an onboarding record must be traceable: who changed it, when, and
   what the prior value was. The audit history is retained for seven years.

5. Records must remain exportable in an open format. The organisation retains
   ownership of its data and of any system built to hold it.

6. Where a statutory employment requirement applies, the statutory requirement takes
   precedence over any process convenience.
