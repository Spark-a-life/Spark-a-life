## 1. Executive Summary

The source describes how a frontier data and talent business is adapting to agentic AI: training data is migrating from static annotation toward full RL simulation environments, human work is concentrating on ambiguous edge cases, and coding agents have inverted the engineering bottleneck from implementation to judgment.

Proposed disposition is conditional extract. Three items are extracted as load-bearing intelligence: the data progression topology (SFT to preference ranking to rubric annotation to RL environments), the sufficiency versus continuous-reward task taxonomy, and edge-case saturation as a monitorable drift signal. Two items are quarantined as ungraded signals, not planning inputs: the token-spend-exceeds-payroll claim and the architect-to-implementer ratio inversion. All extracted items are governed through existing gates; no new estate machinery is required. One commercial wedge is flagged for deliberate evaluation: governed construction of RL training environments, where auditability is currently absent from the market.

## 2. Strategic Opportunity Map

**O1. Governed RL environment construction (highest potential, highest cost).** Realistic application start-states, multi-file state trees, and mock enterprise environments are the expensive input of the emerging data economy. WiseGen already builds governed, witness-chained, admission-gated environments (v0.9.0 estates, Forge demonstrator). Nobody in the RL-environment market is selling auditability, provenance, or tamper-evidence. A governed environment fabrication capability would be structurally differentiated. Gate through CFO before any build commitment.

**O2. Evaluation drift monitoring as a service.** Edge-case saturation is precisely the failure mode the EFO, RDB and PMS chain was designed to catch. Packaging saturation detection and rubric refresh cadence as a client-facing offer extends existing assets into a market that is only now discovering it has the problem.

**O3. Judgment-first capability curriculum.** The claimed bottleneck inversion (workflow mapping, impact validation and architecture over implementation) maps directly onto the training pathway products for institutional leadership audiences. Use as curriculum material only after the underlying hypothesis is tested (see R2 in the Risk Register).

**O4. Engagement scoping instrument.** The sufficiency versus continuous-reward taxonomy is immediately usable in APE Intelligence discovery: classify each client workflow at intake, since the two classes need different governance, different evaluation designs, and different pricing logic. Lowest cost, fastest deployment of the four.
